# Search

Search your library, use advanced search (Pro), and save a search to the sidebar.

Tap the magnifying glass at the top of Library or Feeds.

![The search field, with the Advanced button underneath.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/search-field.png)

## Search as you type

Start typing, and the list narrows to matching articles on your device straight away. That works with no connection.

To look through every word of every article, tap **Search** on the keyboard. Readstow asks the server, so it needs a connection. Your recent searches show under the field while it’s empty. Tap one to run it again.

You can use a few shortcuts in the search box. For example:

  - `label:Research` finds articles with that label.
  - `is:unread`, `is:pinned` and `is:archived` filter by state.
  - `has:highlights` and `has:notes` find articles you’ve marked up.
  - `title:`, `author:` and `site:` look in one place, such as `site:example.com`.
  - `in:inbox`, `in:archive` and `in:following` choose where to look.

Put them together, such as `label:Research is:unread`.

## Advanced search (Pro)

Prefer a form? Tap **Advanced** under the search field. Fill in as little or as much as you like, then tap **Search**.

![The Advanced search form: words, where to look, feeds, labels and read state.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/search-advanced.png)

  - **Words** can be anywhere in the article, only in the title, or from an author or a site.
  - **Look in** can be Everywhere, Library, Feeds or Archived.
  - **Feeds** and **Labels** narrow it to the ones you pick.
  - **Read**, **Has highlights** and **Has notes** narrow it by state.
  - **Saved** picks a time: today, this week, this month, this year, or between two dates.

On the Free plan, **Advanced** is part of Pro. The search box and its shortcuts work on every plan.

## Save a search

When a search gives you a list you’ll want again, tap the bookmark button at the top right of the results. Give it a name and tap **Save**. It then appears under **Saved searches** in the sidebar.

![The Save this search box.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/search-save.png)

Press and hold a saved search in the sidebar to **Rename…**, **Edit search…** or **Delete** it.
