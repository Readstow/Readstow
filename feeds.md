# Feeds

Follow sites, sort them into folders, manage and pause feeds, and see X posts.

The **Feeds** tab is for skimming. New articles from the feeds you follow arrive here by themselves, along with posts and the newsletters you’ve sent to Feeds.

Open the sidebar (tap the **R**) and look at the **Feeds** section. It has four views:

  - **All feeds** shows everything.
  - **Posts** shows X posts.
  - **RSS** shows articles from the feeds you follow.
  - **Newsletters** shows issues that arrive in Feeds.

If you like an article from Feeds, press and hold it and tap **Keep in Library**. It moves to your Library and stays there.

## X posts

Posts from X appear as post cards. A repost shows a line above it, such as "↻ Alex reposted", then the original post. When a post quotes another, the quoted post shows as a card underneath. To see the post on X, press and hold it and choose **Open original in X**.

## Add a feed

1.  Tap **+**. (You can also use the **+** in **Settings → Manage feeds**, or **Add here** on a folder.)
2.  Type or paste the address of a site, a page or a feed. Readstow checks it for a moment, then shows what you can do with it.
3.  Tap **Follow**. If the site has several feeds, each one has its own **Follow**.

You can also paste a link to a profile or channel on Reddit, YouTube, Apple Podcasts, SoundCloud, Mastodon, Bluesky, Micro.blog, Pixelfed or Flickr. Readstow finds its feed.

![The + menu in Feeds: Save a link and Add a feed.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/feeds-add-menu.png)

With Pro, pick a **Folder** for the feed and any **Labels** you’d like every new item to get on the same screen, before you follow. From a folder’s **Add here**, that folder is already chosen. After you follow, the feed opens and its posts load in.

![Follow this feed, with the feed found, a Folder and Labels.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/feeds-add-found.png)

New items arrive in Feeds. The Free plan follows up to 3 feeds. Pro follows up to 250.

## Folders (Pro)

Folders group your feeds in the sidebar, with a count beside each. A folder can hold other folders. The **Library** section has folders of its own, for your saved articles.

![The Feeds section of the sidebar, with the folders Technology, AI inside it, and Law, each with its feeds.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/feeds-sidebar-categories.png)

  - Tap the folder button next to **Feeds** to make a new folder.
  - Tap the arrow next to a folder to open or close it.
  - Tap a folder or feed to see only its items.

Press and hold a folder for more:

![The menu on a folder: Add here, New folder inside, Rename, Move to, Auto-label and Delete.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/feeds-sidebar-category-menu.png)

  - **Add here** opens the add screen with this folder already chosen.
  - **New folder inside…** and **Rename…** do what they say.
  - **Move to** shifts a folder to the top level, or inside another.
  - **Auto-label…** makes a rule for the folder. See [Labels, folders, rules and Auto-archive / delete](organising.md#rules-pro).
  - **Delete…** asks first. What’s in the folder moves up a level, and nothing else is deleted.

Press and hold a feed for its menu:

![The menu on a feed: Edit feed, Rename, Move to, Pause, Mark all read, Auto-label and Delete.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/feeds-sidebar-feed-menu.png)

  - **Edit feed…** opens all of the feed’s settings.
  - **Move to** puts the feed in a folder, or in a new one. **None** takes it out of its folder.
  - **Pause** stops checking the feed. **Resume** starts again.
  - **Mark all read** marks everything in the feed read.

On the Free plan, the sidebar lists your feeds without folders, and a feed’s settings say that folders and rules are part of Pro.

## Manage feeds

**Settings → Manage feeds** lists every feed and newsletter, with how many items each has and when the latest arrived. Paused ones are in their own section. A feed that has a problem says so. Manage feeds needs a connection.

![Manage feeds: Active and Paused sections, with each feed’s item count.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/feeds-manage.png)

Swipe a row to the left to **Pause** or **Delete** it.

![A feed swiped to the left, showing Pause and Delete.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/feeds-manage-swipe.png)

Tap a feed to change its settings.

![A feed’s settings: name, where it arrives, what to save, folder, rules, pause and address.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/feeds-settings.png)

  - **Name.** Rename it however you like.
  - **Arrives in.** **Feeds** or **Library**.
  - **Save.** **The full article**, **Only the feed’s text**, or **Full article if the feed sends a summary**. Some feeds send only the first lines. The last choice fetches the whole page in that case, and keeps the feed’s own text otherwise.
  - **Folder** and **Rules** (Pro).
  - **Pause this feed** stops checks. Items you already have stay.
  - **Address.** Fix a mistake, or a feed that moved.
  - **Delete this feed** asks whether to keep its items or delete them too. Deleted items stay in Deleted for 30 days.

Tap **Save** at the top when you’ve changed the name, where it arrives, what to save, or the address. Folder, Pause and Delete take effect straight away.
