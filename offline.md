# Offline reading and storage

How Readstow downloads your library, what it uses on mobile data, and how to keep an eye on storage.

Everything you save downloads to your device in full, text and pictures, on every plan. Once an item has downloaded, it opens with no connection. The **Downloaded** view in the sidebar lists everything that’s on your device now.

Changes you make offline, such as archiving or deleting, wait on your device and are sent when you’re next connected. Under Sync on Profile you’ll see the status and when it last synced. Pull down on Profile to sync straight away.

If an item hasn’t finished downloading, open it while you’re online and Readstow fetches it.

## Mobile data and storage

Go to **Settings → Mobile data and storage**.

![Mobile data and storage: how to use mobile data, offline storage and what to do when it’s full.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/settings-data.png)

**On mobile data**

  - **Check for new items.** **Automatically**, or **Only when I pull to refresh**. Your own changes are always sent.
  - **Download.** **Text only** keeps images for Wi-Fi. **Text and images** downloads both.
  - **Mobile data this month** shows what Readstow has used.

An article you open always downloads in full, and on Wi-Fi everything does.

**Storage**

  - **Offline storage** sets how much room Readstow may use: 500 MB, 1 GB, 2 GB, 5 GB (the usual), 10 GB, or no limit.
  - **When storage is full.** **Ask me**, or let Readstow clear **Images of oldest items** (the text stays) or **Oldest items** (they leave this device, stay in your library, and download again when you open them). Recent and pinned items are left alone.
  - **Free up space…** clears room now.
  - **Used** and the on-device total show how much is stored.

## Signing out

Signing out removes your downloads from that device. Your library stays in your account and comes back when you sign in again.
