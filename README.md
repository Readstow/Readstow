# Readstow User Guide

How to use every part of Readstow, with pictures of the places that are easy to miss.

Readstow is a read-it-later, RSS feed and newsletter reading app by DexWorks Pty Ltd. Website: [readstow.com](https://readstow.com). The same guide is on the website at [readstow.com/guide](https://readstow.com/guide).

## Contents

1. [Getting started](getting-started.md): Make an account, find your way around Readstow, and save your first article.
2. [Saving](saving.md): Save a page from Safari or any app, follow a feed, or paste a link into Readstow.
3. [Your Library](library.md): The Library’s views, chips, sidebar counts, sorting, Jump to a date, the press-and-hold menu, swipes, and picking several articles at once.
4. [Feeds](feeds.md): Follow sites, sort them into folders, manage and pause feeds, and see X posts.
5. [Newsletters](newsletters.md): Get newsletters into Readstow with your own inbox address, and manage each sender.
6. [Reading](reading.md): The reader’s menu and display settings, finding words, highlights and notes, links in an article, and making a book of your articles.
7. [Search](search.md): Search your library, use advanced search (Pro), and save a search to the sidebar.
8. [Labels, folders, rules and Auto-archive / delete](organising.md): Tidy your library with labels and folders, let rules label new items for you, and clear out old articles automatically.
9. [Bringing your library over](import.md): Import your saved links, bookmarks and feeds from another app, a browser or a spreadsheet, and export your whole library.
10. [Offline reading and storage](offline.md): How Readstow downloads your library, what it uses on mobile data, and how to keep an eye on storage.
11. [Account and plans](account-and-plans.md): Your account, password and devices, the Free and Pro plans, and deleting your account.
12. [FAQ](faq.md): Quick answers to the questions people ask most about Readstow.
