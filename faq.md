# FAQ

Quick answers to the questions people ask most about Readstow.

## About Readstow

### What is Readstow?

Readstow is a read it later app. It keeps the articles you save, the sites you follow and the newsletters you love. Every article is saved to your device in full, pictures included, so you can read it without a connection.

### Is Readstow free?

Yes, there’s a Free plan. It keeps up to 100 saved articles and follows 3 feeds. Pro lifts the limits and starts with a month free. The App Store shows Pro’s price in your currency. See [Account and plans](account-and-plans.md).

### How does Readstow save an article?

When you save a link, Readstow downloads the page and keeps the article itself: its text and pictures, without the site’s menus and ads. The article is then stored on your device, so it opens instantly, even offline. If a site blocks the download, Readstow can try loading the page on your device instead. See [Saving](saving.md).

## Saving and reading

### Readstow isn’t in my share sheet.

Tap Share, scroll to the end of the row of apps, tap **More**, and move Readstow up. See [Saving](saving.md).

### Can I read without a connection?

Yes, on every plan, Free included. Everything you save downloads in full, text and pictures, so it opens with no signal. If an item hasn’t finished downloading yet, open it while you’re online. See [Offline reading and storage](offline.md).

### A page won’t save. What can I do?

Readstow tells you why. Tap **Open Original** to read it on the web, or have Readstow try loading the page on your device, which sends what it saw. If it keeps failing, email <support@readstow.com> with the link.

### Can I read paywalled sites I subscribe to?

Yes, with Pro. Open an article from a site you pay for and tap **Connect**. The site’s own sign-in page opens inside Readstow. Sign in as you normally would, and the article opens in full. Your sign-in stays on your device. On the Free plan you see the preview. See [Sites you subscribe to](reading.md#sites-you-subscribe-to-pro).

### How do I get an article back after deleting it?

Open the sidebar and tap **Deleted**. Items stay there for 30 days. Swipe right on one, or press and hold, and tap **Recover**.

### Where did my finished articles go?

Archive moves them out of your Inbox but keeps them. Open the sidebar and tap **Archived**.

### How do I highlight or add a note?

Press and hold a word in the article, drag to select, then tap **Highlight** or **Note**. All your notes are under **Notes** in the sidebar. See [Reading](reading.md).

### Can I change the text size and the colours?

Yes. **Settings → Display** has the font, size, margins, line height and themes, including Sepia and Black. See [Reading](reading.md).

## Feeds and newsletters

### How do I follow a site?

Tap **+** in Feeds, then **Add a feed**, and type the site’s address. Readstow finds the feed. See [Feeds](feeds.md).

### How do I pause or delete a feed?

Press and hold it in the sidebar, or go to **Settings → Manage feeds** and swipe it to the left.

### Where do I find my newsletter address?

**Settings → Newsletter inbox**. Tap **Copy address**. See [Newsletters](newsletters.md).

### My mail service asks me to confirm forwarding. Where’s the code?

It appears at the top of **Settings → Newsletter inbox**, with a **Confirm forwarding** button and a code you can copy.

### An email I expected isn’t in Readstow.

Look under **Held messages** on the Newsletter inbox page. Mail that doesn’t look like a newsletter, or comes from a sender you’ve paused or blocked, waits there. Tap it, then **Save and allow this sender** to let that sender in from now on.

### Can I send newsletters to Feeds instead of my Library?

Yes. Change **Arrives in** on one newsletter’s settings. New newsletters start in your Library.

## Organising

### What’s the difference between a label, a folder and a rule?

A folder groups your feeds or saved articles in the sidebar, a label says what it’s about, and a rule applies labels (and more) to new items for you. See [Labels, folders, rules and Auto-archive / delete](organising.md).

### Can I put a saved article in a folder?

Yes, with Pro. Press and hold it in your Library and tap **Folder…**.

### How do I see only the articles I haven’t read?

Tap the **Unread** chip under the title.

### Can I change the chips under the title?

Yes. Press and hold a chip, or go to **Settings → Lists → Filter buttons**. You can have up to five, **All** included. See [Your Library](library.md#chips).

### Can I jump to an older date in a long list?

Yes. Tap the sort button, then **Jump to a date…**. It works when the list is in date order.

### Can I choose what swiping does?

Yes. **Settings → Swipes**.

## Plans and account

### What does Pro add?

Unlimited saved articles (Free keeps up to 100), up to 250 feeds (Free follows 3), folders, rules, Auto-archive / delete, advanced search, import, books for your Kindle, and the sites you subscribe to in full. See [Account and plans](account-and-plans.md).

### What happens if my Pro plan ends?

Your account becomes a Free account. Everything you saved stays readable and nothing is deleted. New saves stop above the Free limit, and feeds above it pause.

### I forgot my password.

Tap **Forgot password?** on the sign-in screen and Readstow emails you a reset link.

### How do I see where I’m signed in?

**Profile → Account → Signed-in devices**. You can sign out any of them.

### Why was I signed out on my device?

You can be signed in on two devices. If you sign in on a third and choose this one to sign out, it signs out the next time it connects. See [Account and plans](account-and-plans.md#two-devices).

### How do I delete my account?

**Profile → Account → Delete account**. It deletes your library, highlights, feeds and account for good. Copies in backups are gone within 14 days.

### Do you track me?

No. The app has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics. See [Trust and security](https://readstow.com/trust).

### How do I bring my library from another app?

Import is part of Pro. Go to **Settings → Import from another app** and choose your export file. See [Bringing your library over](import.md).

### Can I take my library with me?

Yes, on every plan. Go to **Settings → Export my library**. You get one ZIP with every item as a web page with its images, your highlights and notes, and your feeds. Open `index.html` in any browser. See [Bringing your library over](import.md#export-your-library).

### Can I read my articles on a Kindle?

With Pro, yes. Press and hold an article, tap **Book…**, then **Send to Kindle**. Add your Kindle address first, in **Settings → Send to Kindle**. See [Reading](reading.md#send-to-a-kindle-or-save-as-a-book).

## Still stuck?

Email <support@readstow.com>. Say which device and system version you have, and what you expected to happen. A person replies, usually within two working days. You can also tap **Send diagnostics** on Profile and mention it in your email. See [Support](https://readstow.com/support).
