# Saving

Save a page from Safari or any app, follow a feed, or paste a link into Readstow.

## From Safari or another app

1.  In Safari, or any app with a Share button, tap **Share**.
2.  Choose **Readstow** in the row of apps. If you don’t see it, scroll to the end of the row, tap **More**, and move Readstow up.
3.  A card opens. Readstow first works out whether the link is an article or a feed, then shows only what fits. Nothing is saved until you tap.

![The row of apps in the share sheet, with Readstow at the end.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/share-sheet-apps.png)

In Mail or Messages, press and hold a link, then tap **Share**.

### An article

An article goes to your Library. The card lets you decide a few things first. All of them are optional.

![The options on the Readstow share card: a folder and your labels.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/share-card-options.png)

  - **Folder.** Appears if you have Pro and have made folders.
  - **Labels.** Tap as many as you like. Tap again to take one off. To make a new one, tap **New label…** at the start of the row, type a name, then tap **Add** or Return. The label is made and put on this save.

Then tap one of these:

  - **Save** keeps the article in your Library.
  - **Open now** saves it and opens it in Readstow.
  - **Cancel** closes the card and saves nothing.

### A feed

An RSS or Atom feed, a site’s front page, a Reddit community, or a YouTube channel or playlist can only be followed. The card offers **Follow** (with a folder and labels, if you have Pro) and **Cancel**. Follow opens that feed in Feeds. If the feed can’t be followed, the card says why.

### Other things the card can tell you

  - **It will download when you are back online.** You were offline. Readstow keeps the link and fetches the article as soon as you’re connected.
  - **Sign in first.** Open Readstow, sign in, then share again.
  - **Kept for later.** You’ve used the Free plan’s room for saved articles. The link is kept and saved when you move to Pro.

## Paste a link into the app

In Library or Feeds, tap **+**, then paste or type the link. If you’ve just copied a link, Readstow fills it in for you. Readstow works out whether it is an article or a feed. An article is saved to your Library: add a **Folder** (Pro) and **Labels** if you like (**New label…** makes one on the spot), then tap **Save** or **Open now**. A feed can only be followed, so the sheet offers **Follow**, or says why it can’t be.

Saving a link works even with no connection. Readstow sends it as soon as you’re back online.

## Save from inside an article

Press and hold a link in an article to see what you can do with it: open it in Readstow, or save it for later. If the link is a feed, you’re offered **Follow** instead. [Reading](reading.md) has the details. A plain tap opens the link in a browser inside the app, so you keep your place.

## If a page can’t be saved

Some pages won’t download. Readstow says why in plain words, for example that the site blocked the download, or that the page couldn’t be found. You can then tap **Open Original** to read it on the web, or have Readstow try loading the page on your device, which sends what it saw. If the page is behind a paywall on a site you pay for, see [Sites you subscribe to](reading.md#sites-you-subscribe-to-pro). If a page keeps failing, email <support@readstow.com> with its link.
