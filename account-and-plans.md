# Account and plans

Your account, password and devices, the Free and Pro plans, and deleting your account.

Open **Profile**. It has four groups: your account and plan, Settings, Sync, and About.

![The top of Profile: your account and plan (a Pro account shows a Pro badge), Subscription and Settings.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/profile-top.png)

  - **Account and plan.** Your email address and plan, and **Subscription**.
  - **Settings.** Everything you set once. See [Getting started](getting-started.md).
  - **Sync.** Whether you’re up to date, when you last synced, **Send diagnostics** and the **Activity log**. Tap the **ⓘ** for how syncing works.
  - **About.** The privacy policy, terms, support and the app’s version.

## Your account

Tap your email address to open **Account**.

![The Account screen: email, change password, signed-in devices and delete account.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/account-screen.png)

  - **Email.** Change your address. Readstow asks for your password and sends a link to the new address to confirm it. If your address isn’t confirmed yet, **Resend verification email** is here too.
  - **Change password.** Enter your current password, then the new one twice. Your other devices are signed out.
  - **Signed-in devices.** See where you’re signed in. Tap **Sign out** beside a device, or **Sign out all other devices**.
  - **Delete account.** See below.

Forgotten your password? On the sign-in screen, tap **Forgot password?** and Readstow emails you a reset link. The link works for 30 minutes.

### Two devices

You can be signed in on two devices at a time. When you sign in on a third, Readstow lists the two devices, with when each was last used. Tap the one to sign out, then confirm. This device signs in, and the one you chose is signed out the next time it connects. If you sign in on another device and your first one shows **Signed out on another device**, that is why.

## Free and Pro

| What                                       | Free      | Pro                       |
| ------------------------------------------ | --------- | ------------------------- |
| Saved articles                             | up to 100 | no limit                  |
| Feeds                                      | up to 3   | up to 250                 |
| Newsletters                                | yes       | yes                       |
| Offline reading, labels, highlights, notes | yes       | yes                       |
| Export your library                        | yes       | yes                       |
| Search                                     | yes       | yes, plus advanced search |
| Folders                                    | no        | yes                       |
| Rules                                      | no        | yes                       |
| Auto-archive / delete                      | no        | yes                       |
| Import                                     | no        | yes                       |
| Books: Save as EPUB, Send to Kindle        | no        | yes                       |
| Read the sites you subscribe to, in full   | no        | yes                       |

Newsletters don’t count towards your feeds. On the Free plan, Profile shows how much of each limit you’ve used:

![A Free account’s line under its email: how many saved articles and feeds it has used.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/profile-free-plan.png)

You only hear about a limit when you reach it. On the Free plan the sidebar shows your feeds without folders, and a feed’s settings say that folders and rules are part of Pro.

### Choose or change a plan

Go to **Profile → Subscription**. It shows your plan and when it renews. **Change plan** lists the plans. There you can also manage your subscription with Apple, restore a purchase, or redeem a code.

![The Subscription screen: your plan, when it renews, and Change plan.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/subscription-screen.png)

If Pro ends, your account becomes a Free account. Everything you saved stays readable. New saves stop above the Free limit, feeds above the Free limit pause, and rules pause. Nothing is deleted.

Billing, renewals and refunds stay with Apple.

## Sign out

**Profile → Sign out** removes your library from that device. It stays in your account. If you have changes that haven’t synced yet, Readstow warns you first.

## Delete your account

1.  Open **Profile → Account**.
2.  Tap **Delete account**.
3.  Enter your password, and type **DELETE** to confirm.

This deletes your library, highlights, feeds and account for good, and can’t be undone. Copies in backups are gone within 14 days. Signing out is different: it only removes your library from that phone.

## Your privacy

Readstow has no ads, no tracking and no third-party analytics in the app. The [Trust page](https://readstow.com/trust) explains how your account is looked after, and the [privacy policy](https://readstow.com/privacy) has the full detail.

If something goes wrong, tap **Send diagnostics** under Sync on Profile. It sends app events and error codes, not your articles or password, and only when you choose to send it. The **Activity log** shows the same events in plain text.
