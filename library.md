# Your Library

The Library’s views, chips, sidebar counts, sorting, Jump to a date, the press-and-hold menu, swipes, and picking several articles at once.

The Library holds what you’ve saved and what you’ve chosen to keep. It’s the place for things you mean to read.

## The views

Tap the **R** at the top left to open the sidebar. The first section lists your Library views.

![The Library section of the sidebar: Inbox, Downloaded, Archived, Highlights, Notes, Newsletters and Deleted.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/library-sidebar-views.png)

  - **Inbox** is what you’ve saved and not archived yet.
  - **Downloaded** is everything stored on this device, ready to read offline.
  - **Archived** is what you’ve finished with but want to keep.
  - **Highlights** is every article that has a highlight or a note.
  - **Notes** lists every note you’ve written. See [Reading](reading.md#all-your-notes).
  - **Newsletters** is the issues that arrived by email.
  - **Deleted** is what you’ve removed. Items stay here for 30 days. Swipe right on one, or press and hold, to **Recover** it. **Delete now** clears it for good.

A number beside each view counts what’s in it. By default an Inbox shows "unread/total", for example 2/40. To change that, go to **Settings → Lists → Sidebar counts** and choose **Unread of total**, **Unread only** or **Total only**.

![The Sidebar counts choice in Settings: Unread of total, Unread only, Total only.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/settings-sidebar-counts.png)

## Chips

Under the title there’s a row of chips. They narrow the list you’re in, in Library and Feeds alike. You start with three. **All** shows everything in the view. **Unread** shows what you haven’t started: articles you haven’t marked read and haven’t read any real part of. **Pinned** shows the articles you’ve pinned. Tap a chip again to see the whole list.

The chips are yours to choose. Press and hold any chip, or go to **Settings → Lists → Filter buttons**. You can have up to five, **All** included. Add Downloaded, Highlights, Long reads, Today, one of your labels or a saved search, drag to reorder, and tap the red minus to remove one.

![Filter buttons: your chosen chips with a minus beside each, and the chips you can add.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/settings-filter-buttons.png)

## Sort, and jump to a date

Tap the sort button (the up and down arrows) to choose an order.

![The sort menu: Newest saved, Oldest saved, Recently read, Recently published, Longest, Shortest, and Jump to a date.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/library-sort-menu.png)

When the list is in date order (Newest saved, Oldest saved or Recently published), the menu ends with **Jump to a date…**. Pick a day on the calendar and tap **Jump**. The list scrolls to where that day starts. If nothing was saved that day, it lands on the nearest day.

![The Jump to a date calendar.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/library-jump-to-date.png)

## Press and hold an article

Press and hold any article, in the Library or in Feeds, for its full menu.

![The menu on an article: Select, Mark read, Reset read position, Pin, Labels, Folder, Archive, Open original, Share, Copy link and Remove.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/library-item-menu.png)

  - **Select** starts picking several articles. See below.
  - **Mark read** or **Mark unread**. If you’ve read part of an article, **Reset read position** takes it back to the top.
  - **Pin** or **Unpin**.
  - **Keep in Library** appears on items from Feeds. It moves the item to your Library so it stays.
  - **Labels…** opens your labels so you can tick the ones that fit.
  - **Folder…** puts an article from your Library in one of your folders (Pro). Pick **New folder…** to make one on the spot, or **No folder** to take it out.
  - **Archive** or **Unarchive**.
  - **Open original** opens the page on the web. **Share** and **Copy link** do what they say.
  - **Book…** makes an EPUB of the article, or sends it to your Kindle (Pro). See [Reading](reading.md#send-to-a-kindle-or-save-as-a-book).
  - **Remove** moves it to Deleted.

![The Folder submenu, listing New folder and your folders.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/library-item-category-menu.png)

## Swipes

Swipe a row to the left or right for quick actions. By default, swiping right in the Library pins, and swiping left archives or deletes. A short swipe shows both buttons. A long swipe runs the first one.

![A row swiped to the left, showing Delete and Archive.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/library-swipe-left.png)

You can choose what each swipe does for the Library and for Feeds in **Settings → Swipes**.

![The Swipes settings: a picture and two choices for each direction.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/settings-swipes.png)

## Pick several at once

1.  Tap **☰**, then **Select**. (Or press and hold an article and tap **Select**.)
2.  Tap the articles you want. **Select All** picks everything in the view.
3.  Use the bar at the bottom: **Archive**, **Labels**, **Mark read** or **Mark unread**, **Book** (Pro), or **Delete**.

![The bar at the bottom while picking: Archive, Labels, Mark read, Book and Delete.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/library-select-bar.png)

Tap **Done** when you’ve finished.
