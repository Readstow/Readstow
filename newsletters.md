# Newsletters

Get newsletters into Readstow with your own inbox address, and manage each sender.

Readstow gives you a private email address. Mail sent to it arrives in Readstow as an article you can read offline. Each newsletter becomes a source of its own, like a feed.

## Find your address

Go to **Settings → Newsletter inbox**. Your address is at the top. Tap **Copy address** to copy it.

![The Newsletter inbox page: your address, a Copy address button, and the senders that have written to it.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/newsletters-page-top.png)

The address only receives mail. It can’t send anything.

## Get newsletters to it

Subscribe to the newsletter with your Readstow address instead of your usual one.

If a newsletter won’t accept your Readstow address, subscribe with your own email and forward its mail to your Readstow address. Some mail services send a confirmation first. It appears at the top of the Newsletter inbox page, with a **Confirm forwarding** button and a code you can copy.

## Forward or send a single email

To send one issue, forward it to your Readstow address from any mail app. Readstow takes the original sender and subject, not yours, so it appears under the newsletter that sent it.

  - **An email that is just a link** saves that link as an article.
  - **Mail you send yourself.** Email your Readstow address from your own account address and it is saved, even if it isn’t a newsletter.

## Each sender’s settings

Under **Senders**, the five with the newest mail are listed. Tap **Show all senders** to see every one, and search by name or address. Tap a newsletter to open its settings. The same settings are on **Settings → Manage feeds**, or press and hold the newsletter in the sidebar and choose **Edit newsletter…**.

![A newsletter’s settings: name, sender address, where it arrives, folder, rules, and the Pause, Unsubscribe and Block buttons.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/newsletter-sender-settings.png)

  - **Name.** Rename it. Tap **Save** to keep the change.
  - **From** is the sender’s address.
  - **Arrives in.** **Feeds** or **Library**. New newsletters start in Library, which is for newsletters you mean to read. Feeds is for skimming.
  - **Folder** and **Rules** (Pro) work as they do for feeds.
  - **Pause this newsletter** holds its mail until you tap **Resume**. Issues you already have stay.
  - **Unsubscribe from the list…** asks the sender to take your Readstow address off the list, and stops the newsletter. Some senders only offer a link. In that case Readstow opens it so you can finish there.
  - **Block this sender…** holds its mail for good. **Unblock this sender** brings it back.
  - **Delete this newsletter** removes it, and asks whether to keep its issues.

## Held messages

Not every email to your address is a newsletter. A receipt, a personal note from someone else, or mail from a sender you’ve paused or blocked, is held instead of saved. It’s listed under **Held messages** on the Newsletter inbox page (**Settings → Newsletter inbox**), with the reason.

![The Held messages list: a shop’s order email, and a message from a paused sender.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/newsletters-set-aside.png)

Tap a message to choose.

![The choices for a held message: Save it, Save and allow this sender, and Delete.](https://readstow.com/guide/images/newsletters-set-aside-menu.png)

  - **Save it** saves this message as an article.
  - **Save and allow this sender** saves it, and lets mail from that sender in from now on. If you’d paused or blocked them, that’s undone.

<!-- end list -->

  - **Delete** removes this message. You can also swipe it left. **Clear all** removes every held message.

Held messages are deleted after 7 days.
